About The Movie

The life of teenager Jake Portman changes forever when he discovers that the fantastical stories told by his grandfather are real. His journey leads him to Miss Peregrine's Home for Peculiar Children, where he meets children with extraordinary abilities and uncovers secrets that span time itself.
