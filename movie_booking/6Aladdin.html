About The Movie

A thrilling live-action adaptation of Disney's classic tale. Aladdin, a kind-hearted street thief, discovers a magical lamp containing a powerful Genie. With three wishes at his disposal, he sets out to win the heart of Princess Jasmine while stopping the evil Jafar from taking control of Agrabah.
